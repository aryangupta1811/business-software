## Module 04: Suppliers & purchases

File: `docs/requirements/04-suppliers-purchases.md`. Already decided elsewhere:

- After opening stock, stock goes up only through purchase entries (`01` #10).
- Batch, expiry and IMEI per item where the business type needs them (`01` #5, #7); medical profit uses the batch's own cost (`01` #15).
- An inter-state branch transfer makes an IGST invoice at the sending branch and a matching purchase entry at the receiving one (`01` #18).
- Purchase price is hidden from staff unless the owner allows it (`01` #1, the `items.view_cost` switch).
- Supplier dues in the alerts and the daily summary, as digests (sitting 2, `07` A1 and its "supplier dues wait for Module 04").
- **Built already (P1.8):** stock is a ledger; a purchase is a `purchase` movement at the bill's unit cost, which updates the branch's average cost with the formula already in `packages/shared`. Nothing in P1.8 changes for purchases.

### 04 Q1: purchase orders, or only purchase entries on arrival?

**Recommended:** only purchase entries when the goods arrive, in v1. Purchase orders go to `ideas-backlog.md`.

**Why:** small shops order by phone or WhatsApp and record the supplier's bill when the goods come. Orders would add a second document with its own states (sent, part-received, closed) and its own screens, for a step most of these shops don't do on a computer.

**Effect:** keeps P2.1 to one session (Day 7).

### 04 Q2: read a photo or PDF of a supplier bill?

**Recommended:** later, not in v1. Into `ideas-backlog.md` as a strong candidate after Phase 3.

**Why:** it's a good portfolio feature (Claude can read a bill image), but it's a second AI feature with its own accuracy problem: every misread number becomes wrong stock and a wrong supplier balance, so it needs a review screen and its own eval set. The $15 a month (D3) is also sized for the assistant. Better to finish the assistant (Days 8–9) first and add bill reading on Day 10 only if everything else is done.

**Effect:** none on the schedule now. *Your call: if you want it as the one Day 8 idea instead of D6's picks, say so.*

### 04 Q3: track GST paid on purchases (input tax credit)?

**Recommended:** store it, report it, don't reconcile it.

- Each purchase line keeps the GST exactly as printed on the supplier's bill (taxable value, CGST, SGST or IGST, rate), with the supplier's GSTIN, bill number and date.
- The CA export (P1.17's GST summary export, `02` #18) gains a **purchase register** sheet: every purchase bill with its GST, by rate.
- **Not in v1:** matching purchases against what suppliers filed on the GST portal (GSTR-2B), which decides how much credit can actually be claimed. That needs the portal's data and is the CA's job.
- A composition business can't claim input tax credit; its purchases are stored the same way, and the register says so.

**Why:** a CA needs the purchase register every month, and the app already holds every number for it. Reconciliation is a separate product.

**Effect:** a few columns on purchase lines (P2.1) and one more sheet in P1.17's export.

### 04 Q4: who can enter purchases?

**Recommended:** the owner, and staff the owner switches "enter purchases" on for (starts **off**, `00` #17). Paying suppliers is a separate switch that also starts off. A staff member who enters purchases sees the costs they type, but the rest of the app still hides purchase price unless `items.view_cost` is on.

**Why:** in many shops a trusted staff member receives goods and checks the bill; paying money out is a different level of trust.

**Effect:** two permission names in P2.1 and P2.2; P1.5 lists them with the other switches.

### Claude's additions (proposed)

- **B1. Last purchase price.** Saving a purchase sets the item's purchase price field to that line's unit cost, shown as "last purchase price". Profit still uses the average cost (`01` #4, P1.8); the field is only a reference and the default for the next opening stock or count.
- **B2. New MRP or selling price on the bill.** When a line's MRP differs from the item's, the entry offers to update the item's MRP and selling price in the same screen (with batches, P1.10, the batch keeps its own MRP). A warning shows when the cost is above the selling price.
- **B3. The same bill entered twice is refused.** Supplier + their bill number is unique within the business, so the same bill can't double the stock by mistake.
- **B4. A saved purchase is never edited** (like bills, `02` #14). A mistake is fixed with a purchase return, or by cancelling the entry with a reason (owner, or a staff member switched on for it); stock goes back out with the log entry.
- **B5. Paying at entry.** Paid now (cash, UPI, bank, cheque), on credit (adds to the supplier's balance), or split, like a sale (`02` #11).
- **B6. Due dates.** Each supplier has credit days (default 30, owner sets per supplier). A bill is due that many days after its date. The alert says "due in 3 days" and "overdue", with the 3 days settable.
- **B7. Branch transfers.** The receiving branch's "purchase entry" for an inter-state transfer (`01` #18) is an ordinary purchase from a supplier that is the other branch. P1.11 (Day 5) does the stock side; the purchase side lands with P2.1 (Day 7). (This is the open item noted under P1.11 in `PROGRESS.md`.)
- **B8. Not in v1:** reverse charge on purchases from unregistered suppliers (rare for goods), freight spread into item cost (landed cost), purchase orders (Q1), bill reading (Q2). Freight and other charges on a supplier bill are recorded as one "other charges" amount on the entry, with its own GST, not added to item costs.

### 08 Q1: date ranges?

**Recommended:** today (the default), yesterday, this week, this month, last month and a custom range. Every comparison is **to the same days of the earlier period**: on 20 November, "this month" is 1–20 November against 1–20 October, not against all of October.

**Why:** comparing part of a month with a whole month always looks like a drop, and an owner who reads that daily stops trusting the screen.

**Effect:** one date helper with tests in P2.9.

### 08 Q2: branch switcher or combined view by default?

**Recommended:** the owner sees **all branches combined** by default, with a switcher to one branch. Staff see only the branch they're logged into. A shop with one branch sees no switcher at all.

**Why:** the owner's first question is "how is the business doing"; per-branch detail is one click away (`00` #13).

**Effect:** P2.10 (Day 7) as planned.

### 08 Q3: which widgets do staff see?

**Recommended:** by default, staff see **their own work and the branch's stock**: their bills and sales today, held bills at the branch, low stock and expiring soon at their branch, their own attendance this month. No shop-wide money totals, profit, udhaar totals, supplier dues, salaries or flags unless the owner switches that widget on for them (`00` #17).

**Why:** a cashier needs to know what to restock and how their day went; shop totals tell staff how much money the shop makes, which many owners keep private (`00` #16).

**Effect:** each widget names the permission it needs, the same way every route does (ADR 0036).

### 08 Q4: can the owner rearrange or hide widgets?

**Recommended:** **hide and show** per widget, for the owner's own dashboard; the order is fixed by the business-type preset. No drag-and-drop in v1.

**Why:** hiding covers "I don't care about that"; drag-and-drop is a week of polish for little use on a counter computer.

**Effect:** one setting list in P2.9.

### Claude's additions (proposed)

- **C1. Days are business days in IST.** "Today" is the business date (P1.8's `businessDate`), so a sale at 23:50 counts today and the seed's backdated history lands on the right days.
- **C2. Sales means net sales.** Cancelled bills (P1.16) are left out, credit notes are taken off. Each total says whether it includes GST ("₹12,400 incl. GST · ₹10,800 before GST").
- **C3. Worked out live.** Each widget runs its own query when the dashboard opens and refreshes every 60 s; no summary tables in v1. Target: the whole dashboard in under 0.5 s on the demo shop's data (P1.20), measured in P2.9.
- **C4. Every number opens its list.** Low stock opens Items filtered to low stock; udhaar due opens the udhaar list; and so on.
- **C5. Top-selling** by money by default, with a switch to quantity, for the chosen dates.
- **C6. Empty states.** A new shop sees "No sales yet today" and a link to Billing, not a row of zeros.

### 09 Q1: voice input in v1, or later?

**Recommended:** later; into `ideas-backlog.md`.

**Why:** the app runs on a counter computer with a keyboard (`00` #23), and the assistant works well typed in Hinglish. Chrome's built-in speech recognition generally sends the audio to Google's servers, which needs a privacy note and a CSP change (*to check if it's ever picked up; newer Chrome versions have on-device recognition for some languages*), and its Hindi accuracy in a noisy shop is unknown. It's also one more thing for the Day 9 eval to cover.

**Effect:** none.

### 09 Q2: can staff use the assistant, and with which limits?

**Recommended:** yes, behind a per-person switch that starts **off** (`00` #17). When it's on, the assistant can see exactly what that staff member can see in the app, because every tool checks the asking person's permissions (ADR 0015). A refusal says what isn't allowed, not why the data exists ("You don't have access to salaries").

**Why:** a cashier asking "what's running low" or "what did I sell today" is useful; the switch keeps the owner in charge of the cost and of who asks.

**Effect:** one permission name in P3.2; the eval set (P3.4) includes staff questions that must be refused.

### 09 Q3: can it take actions, or only answer?

**Recommended:** **answers only** in v1. Every tool is read-only.

**Why:** zero data loss is the top requirement. A model that misunderstands "Rahul ko absent mark karo" (which Rahul? today or yesterday?) would write a wrong record, and checking actions would double the eval work. Answering already shows the hard parts (permissions, exact numbers, Hinglish). If actions come later, each would show a confirm card and go through the same service and entry log as the screen (ADR 0015).

**Effect:** keeps P3.2–P3.5 to Days 8–9.

### Claude's additions (proposed)

- **E1. The cap is enforced by the app.** Spending is counted per business per month from each response's token usage. At 80% the owner sees a warning; at 100% the assistant pauses until the 1st with a clear message. The Console's own spend limit stays as the backstop (D3). The demo business has its own smaller cap (D5).
- **E2. Every answer shows how it was worked out:** the period, the filters and the tools used, in plain words, folded under the answer ("From bills 1–20 Nov, named customers only"). That makes "never invents numbers" checkable.
- **E3. Chats are kept** per person for 90 days so they can scroll back; the owner doesn't read staff chats. A refused question is written to the entry log (`assistant.refused`), so the owner can see someone fishing for salaries.
- **E4. Ambiguity:** it states its assumption and answers ("top customer by money; by visits it's Sunita"), and asks only when the choice would change the answer a lot, such as two staff named Rahul.
- **E5. Dates:** "this month" is the 1st to today in IST; "this year" means the financial year (April–March), stated in the answer.
- **E6. The eval set** (P3.4): about 50 questions with known answers from the demo data, in all three languages, including refusals and ambiguous ones; the pass mark is 90% exactly right with no invented number. Below that, the model or the tools change before release.
- **E7. Private fields never reach the model.** Tools leave out what the person can't see (purchase cost, salaries, phone numbers unless needed), so the model can't repeat it even by mistake. The filter is in the tools, not in the prompt.

## D5: public demo rules and how the demo resets

**Question:** the portfolio has a one-click "Try the demo" (ADR 0016). What may a visitor not do in the demo business, and how is it reset, given the app can't delete rows (ADR 0007) and the server is stopped outside sessions and every night at 23:30 IST?

### D5a: what a visitor may do

**Recommended:**

- **Two buttons:** "Try as the owner" and "Try as a cashier" (a staff login with the usual staff switches), so a recruiter sees permissions working.
- **One shared demo business.** Everyone using the demo sees the same shop. A banner says: "Public demo. Anyone can see what you type; it resets regularly. Don't enter real data."
- **Refused in the demo business** (a server-side flag on the business, checked in the services, with a test for each):
  - changing the password, email or recovery key, forgetting computers, logging others out;
  - creating staff logins or changing permissions (a visitor could lock the next visitor out);
  - sending WhatsApp or email: the outbox shows what *would* be sent, nothing leaves;
  - large imports (Excel limited to 200 rows).
- **Everything else works**, including billing, cancelling, adjusting stock and udhaar, because showing that is the point.
- **The assistant in the demo:** its own cap of **$5 a month** out of the $15, and **10 questions per visitor per hour** (by session, plus the existing per-IP limit), so one visitor can't use up the month.

**Why:** the demo must be useful without letting one visitor break it for the next, cost money, or send messages to real phones.

### D5b: how it resets

**Options:**

- **A. A new demo business per reset; old ones stay (recommended).** The seed builds a fresh demo shop through the app's services (ADR 0016); the demo buttons switch to the newest one; old demo businesses are hidden and archived, never deleted. *Cost:* storage grows by one seed per reset. Rough size: 3 months of a kirana shop (about 7,000 bills, plus their lines, stock movements and log rows) is roughly 30–60 MB (*estimate, measured at P1.20*). Weekly, that's about 2–3 GB a year against RDS's 20 GB.
- **B. Wipe the demo business's rows as the owner role, outside the app.** Smallest database, but it is a delete script on the live database, needing your OK and a fresh backup each time (`CLAUDE.md` rule 7), and the backups keep the deleted rows anyway.
- **C. No reset.** Visitors' changes pile up and the demo history goes stale.

**When (with A):** at most weekly, and **by hand before an interview** (a GitHub Actions run, the same approval as a deploy). Because the server is often off, there's no nightly job: when the worker starts, it resets only if the last reset is more than 7 days old. The seed's history always ends "yesterday", so a fresh reset also means fresh dates.

**Why A:** it keeps the no-delete rule with no exception, and a reset can't damage anything: the worst case is a demo shop that looks odd until the next one.

**Effect:** P1.20 (Day 8): the demo flag, the refusals and their tests, the two buttons, the banner, the reset command and its workflow; a dated note on ADR 0016; the assistant's demo cap in P3.5. About half a session on top of the seed.
