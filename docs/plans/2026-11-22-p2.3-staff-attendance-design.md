# P2.3 Staff records & attendance — Design Spec

- **Date:** 2026-11-22 (fast track, session 31)
- **Status:** Accepted 2026-11-22. The owner took every recommendation in section 8 (1A to 5A); they are `05` #29–#33.
- **Requirements:** Module 05 (DECIDED 2026-11-19): #1 (record fields), #3 (salary changes from the 1st of a month, old rates kept), #5–#9 (the daily register), #25–#27 (who sees and marks, entry log), #28 (what "holidays taken" counts). Module 00 #16, #17 (switches), #19 (staff without a login), #34 (deactivated, never deleted), #35 (one home branch). Module 07 #13 (P2.3 adds the "absent staff" line to the daily summary).
- **ADRs it builds on:** 0007 (no hard deletes), 0008 (paise), 0027 (tenancy, RLS), 0028 (entry log), 0044 (staff switches as changes from defaults), 0045 (the computer decides the branch), 0047 (append-only tables by grants).
- **New ADR (with PR 1):** 0048, attendance stores only the marks people make; every other day takes its default (section 3).
- **Depends on:** P1.5 (`users` staff rows, `STAFF_SWITCHES`, staff screens) and P1.6 (branches, the owner's branch picker), both merged.

## 1. Goal and success criteria

The owner keeps one record per staff member, with or without a login, and marks the whole branch's attendance in under a minute a day. P2.4 then works out salaries from it without asking anything again.

Done when:

1. The owner can add a staff member **without a login** (name, phone, home branch, joining date, salary, weekly off, paid leaves, overtime), and later give that person a login without making a second record (`00` #19).
2. Existing P1.5 logins get the same record fields; their joining date is filled from when the login was made.
3. A salary change starts from the 1st of a month. Old rates stay, and the rate for any month can be read back exactly (`05` #3).
4. The register for a branch and a day lists every active staff member whose home branch it is, each already showing their default (present, weekly off or shop holiday). One tap changes a person's status, late tick or overtime hours (`05` #5, #6).
5. Changing a day before today needs a reason and is in the entry log. Marking today is logged without a reason (`05` #8, #27).
6. Shop holidays live on a calendar. Removing one archives it, it isn't deleted (`05` #7, ADR 0007).
7. Staff see only their own month by default. "Mark attendance" and "See everyone's attendance" are switches that start off (`05` #25, #26).
8. The register shows today's count ("2 absent, 1 on leave"); the same tested function is the daily summary's line in P2.8 (`07` #13).
9. One business never sees another's staff, rates, marks or holidays (a two-business test).

## 2. What P2.3 does and doesn't do

| In P2.3 | Left for |
| --- | --- |
| Record fields, staff without a login, "give a login" | — |
| Salary type and amount with history from the 1st of a month, set by the owner | Salary switches for staff (`salary.view`, `salary.set`, `salary.advance`, `salary.pay`, `05` #26) and the Salary page: **P2.4/P2.5**, where their screens are |
| The register, past-day changes with a reason, shop holidays, a person's month view | Pay rules, paid-leave quota as absent, locking a finalized month (`05` #19): **P2.4** adds the lock check to the register's write path |
| The summary function and its test | Sending it in the daily summary: **P2.8** |
| — | Final settlement slip on deactivation (`05` #4): **P2.5** |

## 3. Data (one migration, `0018` unless another lands first)

### New columns on `users` (staff only; the owner's stay null)

| Column | Type | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `phone` | text, null | normalised like customers' phones (`normalizePhone`) |
| `joining_date` | date | required for staff; backfilled from `created_at`'s business date (IST) |
| `weekly_off_day` | smallint, null | 0 = Sunday … 6 = Saturday; null = no weekly off |
| `paid_leaves_per_month` | smallint, default 0 | 0 to 31 |
| `overtime_rate_paise` | bigint, null | null = overtime off (`05` #15); per hour |
| `version` | integer | already the staff edit pattern; reused |

A person without a login is a `users` row with `role = 'staff'` and no `password_hash` or `username`. The existing checks already allow it.

### `salary_rates` (new, append-only like `bills`, ADR 0047)

`user_id`, `from_month` (date, always the 1st), `kind` (`monthly` or `daily`), `amount_paise` (> 0), `created_by`, `created_at`. The rate for a month is the row with the latest `from_month` on or before it; two rows for the same month: the later one wins. The app role gets `select, insert` only, so an old rate can't be changed (a grant test).

### `attendance_marks` (new)

`user_id`, `branch_id` (the home branch on that day), `date`, `status` (`present`, `half_day`, `absent`, `paid_leave`, `weekly_off`, `holiday`), `late` (bool), `overtime_minutes` (int, 0 to 720), `version`, `updated_by`, `updated_at`. One row per person per day (unique). Checks: `late` and overtime only on `present`; overtime only in 30-minute steps.

**No row means the default** (question 1): `weekly_off` on the person's weekly off day, `holiday` on a shop holiday for that branch, otherwise `present`. A person isn't on the register before their joining date or after the day they were deactivated. One shared, pure function, `dayStatus(person, date, holidays, mark)`, is the only place this rule lives; the register, the month view, the summary and P2.4's pay all use it.

Why only marks, not a row per person per day (ADR 0048): nobody has to save a register every day, a forgotten day still has a sensible answer, and changing someone's weekly off doesn't leave stale rows. A mark is updated in place; the entry log keeps every before and after (ADR 0028).

### `shop_holidays` (new)

`date`, `name`, `branch_id` (null = every branch, question 3), `archived_at`. One live holiday per branch scope per date.

### Entry log (new `ENTRY_ACTIONS`)

`attendance.marked` (today, no reason), `attendance.changed` (a day before today, reason required), `staff.salary_set`, `staff.login_added`, `holiday.added`, `holiday.removed`. Staff record edits reuse `staff.created` / `staff.updated`.

## 4. Permissions

New switches in `STAFF_SWITCHES`, group `attendance`, both off (`05` #25, #26): `attendance.mark` (the register at the computer's branch) and `attendance.view_all` (other people's month views). Seeing your own month comes with `session.own`. Staff records, salary rates and holidays stay owner-only (`staff.manage`, `settings.*`).

## 5. API

| Route | Who | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/staff` (extended) | owner | adds the record fields and the current rate |
| `POST /api/staff` (extended) | owner | `login` becomes optional: no login = a record only |
| `PUT /api/staff/:id` (extended) | owner | record fields, with `version` |
| `POST /api/staff/:id/login` | owner | gives a login to a record without one (reuses P1.5's checks; `409 has_login`) |
| `GET`, `POST /api/staff/:id/salary-rates` | owner | history; a new rate from `YYYY-MM` (question 2) |
| `GET /api/attendance?date=` | owner, `attendance.mark` | the register at the caller's branch (the owner's picker chooses one) |
| `PUT /api/attendance/:userId/:date` | owner, `attendance.mark` | `status`, `late`, `overtimeMinutes`, `version` (null for a default day), `reason` for a past day; `409 changed` with the latest |
| `GET /api/attendance/month?userId=&month=` | own always; others: owner, `attendance.view_all` | each day's status and the month's counts |
| `GET`, `POST /api/holidays`, `POST /api/holidays/:id/remove` | owner | the calendar |

Refused with clear codes: a future date (question 4), a date before joining or after deactivation, a person from another branch, late or overtime on a day that isn't present, overtime for a person with overtime off, a past day without a reason.

## 6. Screens

- **Staff page (P1.5's, extended):** the list shows everyone, with "No login" for records only. Add and edit carry the record fields; a "Can log in" switch shows P1.5's username and password fields. The person page shows salary history ("₹12,000 a month from Nov 2026") with "Change salary", and "Give a login".
- **Attendance register** (`/attendance`, a free `Alt` key from ADR 0026's table, chosen in PR 2): a date (today by default, back to the joining dates), one row per person with status chips, a late tick and overtime hours for those who have it on. A past day asks for the reason once and keeps it for the next taps on that day. The header shows the day's count. "2 of 2 paid leaves used this month" next to a person's leave chip.
- **Month view:** a calendar grid for one person with the month's counts. For staff without `attendance.view_all`, "My attendance" shows only their own.
- **Settings → Shop holidays:** the year's list; add and remove.
- Three languages, light and dark, 1024 px.

## 7. Tests (test-first for every rule)

- `dayStatus`: weekly off, business-wide and one-branch holidays, a mark over a default, before joining, after deactivation, a month of 28, 29, 30 and 31 days.
- The month counts and the summary line: absent, half-day and paid leave counted; weekly offs and holidays not "taken" (`05` #28).
- Salary rates: the rate for a month across several changes, the same month changed twice, `from_month` always the 1st, the month rule from question 2; the app role can't `update` or `delete` a rate.
- Register: today without a reason, a past day without one refused, the default day sent with `version: null`, two counters changing the same mark (`409 changed`), late or overtime refused where they don't apply, another branch's person refused, staff without `attendance.mark` refused, `attendance.view_all` for other people's months, every change in the entry log with before and after.
- Records: a record without a login, then a login given to it (one row, not two), P1.5 logins backfilled with a joining date, holidays archived not deleted, two businesses kept apart.

## 8. Questions for the owner

**Answered 2026-11-22:** the owner took all five recommendations (1A to 5A). They are `05` #29–#33.

Each has a recommended answer. None changes a DECIDED point.

1. **A day nobody marked.**
   - A. It counts as its default: present, or weekly off or shop holiday. **Recommended:** this is what "everyone starts as present" (`05` #5) means, and nobody has to remember to press Save every day.
   - B. It stays "not marked", and P2.4 won't finalize a month that has unmarked days.

2. **Which month a salary change can start from.**
   - A. This month (from its 1st) or any later month. **Recommended:** a raise agreed on the 20th usually covers the whole month in a small shop, and the month isn't paid until it ends.
   - B. Only next month or later.

3. **Shop holidays with more than one branch.**
   - A. A holiday is for every branch by default; the owner can pick one branch instead. **Recommended:** most holidays (Diwali, Holi) close every branch; a local festival can close one.
   - B. Each branch has its own calendar.

4. **Marking days ahead** (a planned leave next week).
   - A. Not in v1: the register works for today and past days. **Recommended:** keeps the fast track's scope small; the owner marks the leave on the day.
   - B. Allowed for future days too.

5. **What is required when adding someone.**
   - A. Name, home branch and joining date. Phone and salary can come later ("Salary not set" shows until then, and P2.4 won't pay without one). **Recommended:** today's P1.5 logins have no salary, and an owner adding a helper in a hurry shouldn't be blocked.
   - B. Salary is required too.
