# P1.18 Udhaar ledger — Design Spec

- **Date:** 2026-11-23 (fast track Day 8, lane 2)
- **Status:** Proposed. Waiting for the owner's answers to section 9.
- **Requirements:** Module 03 (DECIDED 2026-11-17): #1 (named customers only), #2 (running balance), #3 (partial payments any time), #4 (oldest bills cleared first), #5 (30-day due date, per customer), #6 (advance balance used on the next bill), #7 (no interest), #8 and #9 (credit limit, warn or block with the owner's password), #10 and #11 (who gives udhaar, who records payments). Module 02 #10 and #11 (udhaar is a payment mode; the unpaid part of a bill goes to udhaar). Module 00 #15–#17 (staff switches).
- **Left for P1.19:** overdue buckets, "collected this week", "who owes the most", the statement PDF, the reminder list with click-to-chat (`03` #12–#16). P1.18 makes sure each of those is one query over its data.
- **ADRs it builds on:** 0007 (ledger, one transaction, lock order, idempotency), 0008 (paise), 0027 (tenancy, RLS), 0028 (entry log), 0036 (Zod bodies, declared permissions), 0041 (database timeouts), 0044 (staff switches as changes from defaults), 0047 (append-only tables by grants).
- **New ADR (with PR 1):** udhaar is a ledger with one locked balance row per customer, and "oldest bills first" is computed when it's read, not stored (section 4). Number at merge (0050 today).
- **Depends on:** P1.13 (customers, the payment panel, owner approvals) and P1.14 (`POST /api/bills`), both merged.

## 1. Goal and success criteria

A shopkeeper can sell on credit to a known customer, take money back in parts at any time, and always see exactly what each customer owes, without a notebook.

Done when:

1. At the counter, the payment panel has **Udhaar** for the unpaid part of a bill. It needs a customer with a name and a phone number (`03` #1).
2. Every customer has a **running balance**. It equals the sum of their ledger entries, always (a check function, tested like `findStockMismatches`).
3. **Recording a payment** takes a customer, an amount and a mode (cash, UPI, card, cheque). It works for any amount at any time, and a retry never records it twice (`03` #3, #11).
4. Paying more than is owed leaves an **advance**. The next bill can be paid from it (`03` #6).
5. The bill **over the credit limit** warns, or blocks if the owner chose that. When blocked, the owner's password lets that one bill through, and the entry log has it (`03` #8, #9).
6. **Staff** give udhaar only with the switch "Give udhaar" on, and never beyond the limit without the owner's password (`03` #10).
7. Each customer's page lists their bills and payments with the **balance after each one**, and which bills are still unpaid with their due dates, oldest cleared first (`03` #2, #4, #5).
8. One business never sees another's balances (a two-business test).

## 3. Data (one migration, `0019` unless another lands first)

### New columns on `customers`

| Column | Type | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `credit_limit_paise` | bigint, null | null means "no limit set" (question 1); else ≥ 0 |
| `due_days` | integer, default 30 | 0 to 365 (`03` #5) |

Changed through the existing `PUT /api/customers/:id` with `version`; logged in `customer.updated` with before and after.

### `udhaar_accounts` (new): one row per customer who ever had an entry

`customer_id` (unique per business), `balance_paise` (signed: **above 0 the customer owes; below 0 it's an advance**), `updated_at`. It's the row the bill and the payment lock `FOR UPDATE`, like `item_stock` for stock. Created on the first entry (`insert … on conflict do nothing`, then lock).

### `udhaar_entries` (new, append-only like `bills`, ADR 0047)

| Column | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `customer_id`, `branch_id` | the branch where it happened; the balance is business-wide |
| `kind` | `opening`, `bill`, `advance_used`, `payment`, `payment_reversal` (P1.16 adds `bill_cancelled`) |
| `amount_paise` | signed: + owes more, − paid. Checked per kind: `bill`, `advance_used`, `payment_reversal` > 0; `payment` < 0; `opening` ≠ 0 |
| `balance_after_paise` | the running balance, so the ledger page needs no window function |
| `bill_id` | for `bill` and `advance_used` |
| `mode`, `reference` | for `payment`: cash, UPI, card or cheque; a cheque needs its number |
| `reverses_entry_id` | for `payment_reversal`; unique, so a payment is reversed once |
| `due_date` | for every entry above 0: the day it falls due (question 6) |
| `business_date` | the IST day (`06` #7) |
| `request_key` | idempotency, unique per business (ADR 0007 rule 7) |
| `reason` | required for `payment_reversal` and `opening` |
| `created_by`, `created_at` | |

The app role gets `INSERT` and `SELECT` only; the guard's `APPEND_ONLY` list includes it.

### Changes to existing tables

- `PAYMENT_MODES` gains `udhaar` and `advance`. `bill_payments` keeps a row for each, so a bill's payments still add up to its total (`paymentIssues` is unchanged). `tendered` stays cash-only; `reference` isn't allowed on either.
- `businesses` gains `over_credit_limit` (`warn` | `block`, default `warn`), the same shape as `negative_stock` (`01` #12).
- `APPROVAL_KINDS` gains `credit_limit`. `owner_approvals` gains `amount_paise`, the most udhaar the owner allowed on that bill. A check per kind: `discount` needs the two shares; `credit_limit` needs the amount (the shares become nullable).

### Entry log (new `ENTRY_ACTIONS`)

`udhaar.payment_recorded`, `udhaar.payment_reversed`, `udhaar.opening_set`, `credit_limit.approved`. Udhaar given on a bill is already inside `bill.created` (its payments); the log entry adds the balance after.

## 4. How the balance and "oldest bills first" work

**The balance** is `udhaar_accounts.balance_paise`, changed only by one function, `moveUdhaar(tx, …)` in `packages/db`, the way `moveStock` is the only writer of stock. In one transaction it:

1. locks the account row `FOR UPDATE`;
2. checks the rule for the kind (advance available, limit, a payment not reversed twice);
3. adds the entry with `balance_after = balance + amount`, and updates the account.

`findUdhaarMismatches` compares every account with the sum of its entries and its last `balance_after`.

**Lock order** (ADR 0007 rule 6): a bill locks its items' stock rows (sorted by item ID), then the invoice counter, then the one customer account. A payment locks only the account. Every path takes them in that order, so they can't deadlock.

**Oldest bills first** (`03` #4) **is computed, not stored.** Payments aren't tied to bills. When the customer owes B, the unpaid amounts are the newest entries above 0 that add up to B: walk them newest first until B is covered (the last one taken may be partly paid). Everything older is paid. That is exactly "every payment clears the oldest bill first", and nothing has to be updated when a payment comes in. One tested function in `packages/shared`, `unpaidEntries(entries, balance)`, is the only place this rule lives. P1.19's overdue buckets, the reminder's "oldest due date" and the statement all call it.

Why not store which payment cleared which bill: an allocations table has to be kept right through every payment, reversal and cancellation, and a bug there makes the buckets wrong silently. Computing it from the ledger can't drift from the balance.

## 5. At the counter (changes to P1.13 and P1.14)

**Payment panel** (`F10`): two new rows after cheque.

- **Udhaar** (key `5`), shown when a customer is attached. It puts the rest of the bill on the customer's account. Without a name and phone it says "Add the customer's name and phone for udhaar" and `F3` opens the customer box (`03` #1).
- **From advance** (key `6`), shown only when the customer has an advance; at most the advance (question 3).
- The customer box shows the balance ("Owes ₹1,240" or "Advance ₹300") and the credit limit.

**Over the limit** (new balance after this bill above the limit):

| Who | `over_credit_limit = warn` | `= block` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | a warning; the bill saves | the owner's password, for this bill |
| Staff with "Give udhaar" | the owner's password (`03` #10) | the owner's password |
| Staff without it | Udhaar isn't offered | Udhaar isn't offered |

The owner's OK uses the existing approval flow (`POST /api/billing/approvals` with `kind: credit_limit` and the amount, 30 minutes, one bill). A customer with no limit set: see question 1.

**`POST /api/bills`** (inside its transaction, after pricing and before the invoice number):

- `udhaar` or `advance` needs `customerId`; `udhaar` needs the customer's phone; staff need `billing.udhaar` for `udhaar`.
- `advance` is at most the advance available at that moment (re-read under the lock).
- The limit rule above, with the approval's `amount_paise` ≥ the bill's udhaar.
- `moveUdhaar` adds a `bill` entry for the udhaar part and an `advance_used` entry for the advance part, both with this bill's ID.
- New answers: `422 udhaar_needs_customer`, `422 udhaar_needs_phone`, `403 udhaar_not_allowed`, `409 over_credit_limit` (with the balance and the limit), `409 advance_changed` (with the advance now). Each gets a message in three languages.

## 6. API (new routes; Zod bodies in `packages/shared`, declared permissions, ADR 0036)

| Route | Permission | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/udhaar/customers?q=&owing=` | `customers.view` | customers with their balance, limit, oldest due date; `owing=true` only those with a balance |
| `GET /api/udhaar/customers/:id` | `customers.view` | the ledger, newest first, paged; the unpaid entries with due dates |
| `POST /api/udhaar/payments` | `billing.sell` (question 7) | `{ requestKey, customerId, amountPaise, mode, reference? }` → the entry and the new balance, for the receipt |
| `POST /api/udhaar/payments/:id/reverse` | owner only | `{ reason, ownerPassword }`: a `payment_reversal` entry (question 5) |
| `POST /api/udhaar/opening` | owner only | `{ requestKey, customerId, amountPaise, asOf, reason }`, once per customer (question 4) |
| `PUT /api/settings/udhaar` | `settings.manage` | `over_credit_limit` |

New permission and switch: **`billing.udhaar`** ("Give udhaar"), group billing, parent `billing.sell`, **off** by default (`03` #10). Old staff logins get the default, off (ADR 0044).

## 7. Screens (PR 2)

- **Customers** in the sidebar (`Alt+C` if free): the list with balance, limit and oldest due date, a filter "Owes money", search by name or phone.
- **A customer's page:** details (credit limit and due days for `customers.manage`), the balance in large type, the ledger with the balance after each line, the unpaid bills with due dates, **Record payment** (`P`): amount, mode, reference, Enter saves; then "Payment saved, balance now ₹…". The owner sees **Reverse** on a payment and **Opening balance** while the customer has no entries.
- **Billing:** the payment panel rows and the customer box (section 5), the over-limit warning and the owner's password dialog.
- **Settings → Udhaar:** "Over the credit limit: warn / block".
- Three languages; checked in Chrome at 1024 px.

## 8. Tests (test-first for every rule)

- `unpaidEntries`: nothing owed, one bill partly paid, several bills with payments in between, an advance, a reversal bringing an old bill back, an opening balance as the oldest entry.
- `moveUdhaar` and the account: the balance equals the sum of entries after every kind; two counters adding to one customer at once (both counted, no lost update); `findUdhaarMismatches`; the app role can't `update` or `delete` an entry.
- Bills: udhaar without a customer, without a phone; staff without `billing.udhaar`; advance above what's available; advance used by another counter in between (`409 advance_changed`); every cell of the over-limit table; an approval's amount below the bill's udhaar refused; a used approval refused; the bill's payments still add up; a retried bill makes one entry, not two.
- Payments: any amount, overpayment makes an advance, cheque needs its number, a retry with the same key answers the first payment, the same key with another amount answers `422 key_reused`, reversal owner-only with a reason and only once, opening balance once.
- Every change in the entry log; two businesses kept apart.

## 9. Questions for the owner

Each has a recommended answer. None changes a DECIDED point.

1. **A customer with no credit limit set.**
   - A. "No limit set": the owner can give any amount; staff need the owner's password for any udhaar to that customer. **Recommended:** nothing is limited until the owner decides, and staff can't run up a balance the owner never agreed to.
   - B. A business-wide default limit (for example ₹2,000) applies to every customer until changed.

2. **Staff and the credit limit** (`03` #10 says "never beyond a customer's credit limit").
   - A. Over the limit, a staff bill stops at "Needs the owner's OK" and the owner's password lets it through, in warn and block mode alike. **Recommended:** the customer at the counter isn't sent away when the owner is in the shop, and staff still can't decide it alone.
   - B. Staff are refused outright over the limit; only the owner can bill it.

3. **Using an advance.**
   - A. The payment panel offers "From advance ₹300" as a row the cashier picks. **Recommended:** the customer may want to keep the advance, and the cashier says it out loud.
   - B. The advance is used automatically on every bill to that customer.

4. **Udhaar already in the old notebook.**
   - A. The owner enters an **opening balance** per customer, once, with an "as of" date for the due date and a note; it's in the entry log. **Recommended:** a shop moving to the app has old udhaar on day one; without this they'd have to fake a bill.
   - B. Not in v1: start from zero and keep the old notebook until it's cleared.

5. **A payment recorded by mistake** (wrong customer or amount).
   - A. The owner **reverses** it with a reason and their password; the payment stays in the ledger with a reversing line, and the right one is recorded again. **Recommended:** nothing is edited or deleted (ADR 0007), and the shop still has a way out of a typo.
   - B. No correction in v1.

6. **A bill's due date when the customer's due days change later.**
   - A. Fixed when the bill is saved: bill date + the customer's due days at that time. **Recommended:** changing a customer from 30 to 15 days doesn't make old bills suddenly overdue.
   - B. Always worked out from the customer's current due days.

7. **Who records payments** (`03` #11 says all staff can).
   - A. Anyone with "Sell at the counter" (`billing.sell`), with no new switch. **Recommended:** it's what #11 decided; anyone who takes money for a sale can take money for udhaar, and the entry log shows who did.
   - B. A new switch "Record udhaar payments", on by default, so the owner can turn it off for someone.
