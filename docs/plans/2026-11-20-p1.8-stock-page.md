# P1.8 PR 2 — the stock screens

- **Date:** 2026-11-20 (fast track Day 4, session 26, lane 2)
- **Design:** `docs/plans/2026-11-20-p1.8-stock-ledger-design.md`, sections 1 (point 9), 10 (`apps/web`) and 11. Approved by the owner with the day's lanes (one OK).
- **Builds on:** PR #38 (the stock API, `negativeStock` in settings), ADRs 0025–0026 (design system, native `<dialog>`), 0036 (codes, not words, from the API), 0007 rule 7 (idempotency keys).

## What it adds

1. **The page learns the stock permissions.** `GET /api/items/context` gains `canViewStock` (`stock.view`), `canAdjustStock` (`stock.adjust`) and `branchId` (the caller's branch, where opening stock and adjustments land). This is the one API change; until P1.5 gives the web the caller's permissions, the context is where the Items page learns them (its own comment says so).
2. **Items list:** a Stock column at the caller's branch, with the unit, for callers with `stock.view`. A badge on the row: "Low" (warning) at or below the level, "Out of stock" (danger) at 0, "Below zero" (danger) under 0. A "Low stock only" filter sends `low=true`.
3. **Item page, new "Stock" card** (saved goods, `stock.view`), outside the item form like Barcodes, because each change saves at once:
   - one row per branch: quantity in the stock unit, the low-stock level, the badge; average cost and stock value only with `items.view_cost`;
   - at the caller's branch, with `stock.adjust` and the item not archived: "Set opening stock" while `canOpen`, and "Adjust stock";
   - the history at a branch (a branch picker when there are several), newest first, 50 at a time with "Show more": date, what happened (opening or the reason), the change, the balance after, who, the note; the value with `items.view_cost`.
4. **Opening stock dialog** (native `<dialog>`): quantity; unit cost in rupees only with `items.view_cost`, filled in from the purchase price. Without it no cost is sent and the server uses the purchase price (or answers `cost_required`).
5. **Adjust dialog:** Remove stock / Add stock; the reason list follows the direction (Add offers only Count mismatch, 3B); quantity; a note, required for Other. Checked in the browser with the shared `adjustmentSchema` and `stockQuantityProblem` before sending, so errors show under their fields in the chosen language.
6. **Retries (ADR 0007 rule 7, design §7):** each dialog makes one request key when it opens and sends it with every try until one succeeds. A `503 busy` waits for `Retry-After` (at most 3 s) and tries once more with the same key; if still busy it says "Another counter is changing this item. Try again." and keeps the key. `422 key_reused` (the change was already saved under this key, then edited) closes nothing and says so, and the history refreshes.
7. **Refusals in words:** `below_zero` ("You can't remove more than the stock at this branch"), `opening_exists` (the card refreshes; adjust instead), `cost_required`, `whole_units_only`, `wrong_direction`, `archived`, `forbidden`.
8. **Archive confirm (7A):** with `stock.view` and stock left anywhere, the confirm says how much is left at each branch, and that stock and history stay with the item.
9. **Settings, new "Stock" card:** "When a bill would take stock below zero: Warn / Block" (`01` #12), saved through `PUT /api/settings/stock`. Settings is owner-only already (`settings.view`).
10. Every string in `en`, `hi` and `hi-Latn`.

## Tests first

- API (`items.test.ts`): the context's three new fields for the owner and for a staff member without stock permissions.
- Web (`items.test.tsx`, `settings.test.tsx`): the Stock column and badges, the column hidden without `stock.view`, the low filter's request; the stock card per branch, cost hidden without `items.view_cost`, no actions without `stock.adjust`; opening stock shown only while `canOpen` and sending the key and cost; the adjust form's reasons by direction, note required for Other, whole-unit error under the field, `below_zero` in words, a `503` retried once with the same key; the history list and "Show more"; Hindi labels; the archive confirm with stock left; the Settings stock switch saved.

## Decisions made here (not in the approved design)

- **The caller's branch and stock permissions come through the items context**, not the session: the session carries no permissions yet (P1.5), and one request already gates the page.
- **"Out of stock" shows for any goods item at 0**, including one never stocked: true at the counter, and P1.9's import fills real numbers.
- **Busy retry waits at most 3 s** whatever `Retry-After` says, so a confused header can't freeze the dialog.
